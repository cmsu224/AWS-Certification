#### 🏠 Online (home) proctoring vs 🏢 test center

| 🏠 OnVUE at home | 🏢 Test center |

## 4. 📝 Practice exams and reviewing mistakes

| Source | CLF | SAA | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maarek in-course practice exam | ✅ §22 | ✅ §32 | Included in the courses you own |
| AWS Skill Builder **Official Practice Question Set** | ✅ | ✅ | Free (AWS account login), about 20 questions in real exam style |
| This app (full exam mode) | ✅ 65Q/90 min | ✅ 65Q/130 min | Unlimited; mixes all unlocked questions |
| **Tutorials Dojo** SAA-C03 practice exams (Jon Bonso) | — | ⭐ recommended | ~$15; 6 timed sets + review mode, with excellent explanations |

**How to review a practice exam (this is where the learning happens):**

```
  for every question — right AND wrong:
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ 1. Why is the right answer right?  (say it in one sentence)  │
  │ 2. Why is EACH wrong option wrong? (what would make it right?)│
  │ 3. Wrong because… 🟥 didn't know  🟨 misread  🟦 two-way guess │
  │ 4. 🟥 → rewatch that Maarek lecture at 2×, then app drill      │
  │    🟨 → underline keywords: "MOST cost-effective", "LEAST ops" │
  │    🟦 → write the tie-breaker rule down (e.g. SQS vs SNS)      │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The app tracks misses automatically: wrong answers go back to spaced-repetition box 0 and come up again tomorrow.

## 6. 🌧️ Bad-week protocol

Kids get sick. Sleep disappears. That's expected.

```
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  MINIMUM VIABLE DAY = one ⚡ 2-minute review.  That's a win.       │
  │  It keeps the streak and the spaced-repetition schedule alive.   │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Don't cram** to "catch up". The app recalculates the daily pace from what's left. A missed day adds a couple of minutes per day, not a Sunday marathon.
2. **Behind by more than ~2 h?** Skip the optional stuff first: role-plays, SSH-on-other-OS lectures, the optional lab. Watch hands-ons at 2× without doing them.
3. **Behind by more than a week?** Move the exam date in **Settings**, and in your Pearson VUE booking (free up to 24 h before). SAA has the holiday buffer, and the 50 % voucher lasts about 12 months, so moving it costs nothing.
4. **Coming back:** clear the due reviews first (the app caps them per session), then the next lecture block. Don't rewatch what you already finished.
